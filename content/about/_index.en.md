## Profile

Shin Tokiwa. I have worked in the IT industry for over 20 years, since 2001. I have experienced time and again how rewarding it is to solve problems through software and applications. My interest in emerging technology has never faded, and I still keep up with web technology, which has been evolving continuously since the dawn of the Internet.

In 2008 I co-founded ZEALOT Inc., and I am now in my 18th year as its representative director. While my center of gravity has shifted to management, I have never put down the tools of design and implementation. My guiding policy has not changed: technology is only ever a means to solve a problem. I value relationships in which — after genuinely understanding a client's business — I can say so plainly when the best course of action is to do nothing at all.

## Career

- **2001** — Graduated from a technical college, majoring in information systems
- **2001–2007** — Joined a software development company in Kofu, Yamanashi. Worked as a systems engineer and programmer, covering everything from business system development to server setup and network cabling, on projects ranging from small engagements to several-hundred person-month builds
- **2007–2008** — Freelance engineer
- **December 2008 – present** — Co-founded ZEALOT Inc. and became its representative director. In June 2025 the company joined the SMS DataTech Corporation (SDT) group as a wholly owned subsidiary
- **January 2026 – present** — Founded Brightseed Inc. and became its representative director, operating the restaurant "Yoshio no Motsuya" in Yamanashi
